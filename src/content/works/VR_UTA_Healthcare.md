## Overview

Collaborated as a Software Developer and Systems Analyst on team CareVR in partnership with the University of Texas at Arlington College of Nursing. Together, we designed, engineered, and refactored an immersive virtual reality simulation on Meta Quest 2 to train nursing students in end-of-life and palliative care procedures.

## Key Technical & Systems Analysis Highlights

- **Requirements Gathering & Stakeholder Collaboration:** Facilitated requirements sessions with nursing faculty and clinical subject matter experts (SMEs) to translate complex palliative care protocols (SBAR reporting, Glasgow-Coma Scale evaluations, and home hazard assessments) into formal System Requirement Specifications (SRS) and Architectural Design Specifications.
- **Agile/Scrum Development Lifecycle:** Executed 2-week Agile/Scrum sprint cycles, managing backlog user stories, conducting system testing, and delivering bi-weekly feature demonstrations to non-technical nursing stakeholders to ensure 100% compliance with clinical training objectives.
- **Layered Architecture & Codebase Refactoring:** Engineered a three-tiered software architecture (`Managers`, `Game Objects`, and `Data Objects`) in Unity/C#, refactoring legacy codebase components, building custom dialogue/assessment managers, and implementing real-time scoring and error-tracking reporting interfaces.
- **User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Conducted structured user testing rounds with nursing students and faculty to evaluate controller interaction physics, isolate state management bugs, and gather feedback for iterative simulation enhancements.
